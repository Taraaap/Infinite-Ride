Infinite Ride – Rental Policies & Agreement

Our rental policies are designed to provide you with a seamless, transparent, and secure rental experience. We prioritize your safety and satisfaction from the moment of booking to the vehicle's return.

Booking & Payments

  • No upfront payment is required to reserve a vehicle.
  • Full payment and a refundable deposit are required at the time of vehicle pickup.
  • The security deposit must be 200% of the total rental amount.
  • Accepted payment methods: PhonePe or cash at our office counter.

Damage, Deposit & Insurance

  • Security deposit (200% of rental amount) is collected at pickup and refunded after the vehicle is returned in proper condition.
  • Valid identification (Citizenship, Voter ID or Passport) is required and must be submitted at the time of pickup.
  • Insurance is optional but highly recommended. In the event of damage and if no insurance has been opted, the customer is fully liable for all repair costs.

Terms & Conditions

  1. Eligibility & Driver Requirements: Self drive Renters must be 18+ with a valid driving license. Large Travel vehicles require a company-provided driver.
  2. Rental Period & Extensions: The rental period is set at booking. Extensions may be allowed only if you notify us in advance. The extension fee will be double the regular daily rental rate per extra day. Late returns may also incur additional charges.
  3. Security Deposit & Payment: Full rental amount + 200% deposit must be paid at pickup. A valid ID document must also be submitted.
  4. Vehicle Usage & Restrictions: Legal use only. No racing or illegal Activities.
  5. Fuel Policy: Return the vehicle with the same fuel level. Refueling charges will apply otherwise.
  6. Liability & Indemnity: Infinite Ride is not responsible for personal injuries or lost belongings.
  7. Termination of Agreement: Violation of any terms may result in rental termination without a refund.
  8. Emergency Contact: +977-9800000000 | support@infiniteride.com

Rental Contract Summary

This contract is an agreement between Infinite Ride and the customer. By proceeding, you agree to:

  • Comply with all policies and conditions.
  • Use the vehicle responsibly.
  • Accept applicable charges for damages, delays, or violations.

Failure to comply may result in penalties or cancellation of future bookings.
