Rental Policies

Our rental policies are designed to provide you with a seamless and secure rental experience. We prioritize safety, transparency, and customer satisfaction, ensuring a smooth process from booking to return.

Booking & Payments

Customers can book a vehicle without any upfront payment. Payments can be made at the time of vehicle pickup via PhonePe or cash at our company counter. You will receive a booking confirmation with all necessary details.

Rental Duration

The minimum rental period is 1 day. Extensions are allowed, but advance notice is required for availability and billing adjustments.

Vehicle Usage

Only licensed drivers can operate vehicles. Cars are available with or without a driver, while bikes are self-drive only. Travel vehicles (Sumo, Jeep, Bus) require a company-provided driver. Misuse of the vehicle may result in penalties.

Damage & Insurance

Any damages during the rental period will be deducted from the security deposit. Damage caused by natural disasters (earthquakes, floods) is covered by the company. Insurance options are available.

Privacy Policy

At Infinite Ride, we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. Your data is collected only for essential purposes such as booking verification, customer support, and ensuring a seamless rental experience. We use secure encryption methods to store your information and never share your data with third parties unless legally required. Our privacy practices comply with industry standards to ensure your details remain safe and confidential. By using our services, you agree to our privacy terms. If there are any updates to our policy, we will notify you accordingly. Your trust is important to us, and we are dedicated to maintaining transparency in how we handle your information.

Terms & Conditions

By renting a vehicle, you agree to the following terms. Please read carefully before proceeding.

1. Eligibility & Driver Requirements

Self Drive Renters must be at least 18 years old with a valid driving license. Large Travel vehicles require a company-provided driver.

2. Rental Period & Extensions

The rental period is set at booking. Late returns may incur additional charges.

3. Security Deposit & Payment

A security deposit is required. Full payment is made at pickup. Currently, online payments are unavailable.

4. Vehicle Usage & Restrictions

Vehicles must be used legally. Off-road driving, racing, and illegal activities are prohibited.

5. Damage & Insurance

Damage costs will be deducted from the deposit. Insurance is available. Natural disaster damage is covered by the company.

6. Fuel Policy

Vehicles must be returned with the same fuel level to avoid refueling charges.

7. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations 24 hours before pickup are free. Late cancellations may incur charges.

8. Liability & Indemnity

The company is not responsible for personal injuries or lost belongings during the rental period.

9. Termination of Agreement

The company can terminate rentals if terms are violated. No refunds will be provided.
